# Support

* **Questions and bug reports:** open an issue at https://github.com/Olumide-Akindipe/muni-lcc/issues. Include the project file (with sensitive values removed), the command you ran, the full output, and your Python version.
* **Method questions:** say which guideline, section, and edition your question concerns (for example FEMP M&V Guidelines v5.0, Section 5.3.4.1).
* **Response:** MUNI-LCC is maintained by one person. The maintainer aims to acknowledge new issues within 14 days. There is no guaranteed response time and no paid support.
* **Security or data concerns:** do not post utility account numbers or other identifying data in public issues. Contact the maintainer through the email in `AUTHORS.json`.

MUNI-LCC supports analysis; it does not provide engineering certification, legal advice, or a compliance determination for any award.
